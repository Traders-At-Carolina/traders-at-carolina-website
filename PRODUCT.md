# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated for implementation: React, TypeScript, and Vite. This keeps the greenfield public site easy to extend into authenticated member and administrator surfaces while allowing the public content layer to remain framework-local until a backend is selected.

## Users

- Primary: UNC students exploring quantitative finance, club programming, and membership.
- Secondary: potential company partners who want to collaborate with or recruit from the club.
- Returning members need a clear route to the future authenticated member portal.

## Product Purpose

The public website introduces Traders at Carolina, establishes its focus and activity, helps students understand the member experience, publishes events and recruitment information, and gives firms a direct partnership path. Success means a first-time student can understand the club and reach Join, while a potential partner can reach collaboration information in one navigation step.

## Positioning

Traders at Carolina is a student quantitative finance club at UNC-Chapel Hill centered on learning technical finance, competing, and connecting with peers and industry.

## Operating Context

The public experience consists of Home, About & Team, Membership, Events, Partners, Join, and Member Login. Recurring content will eventually be administered through a protected admin system. Public events may include member-only activities when clearly labeled. Recruitment can be upcoming, open, or closed, and the interest form remains available in every state.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public site is multi-page and student-first.
- Join is the persistent primary action; Member Login is a lower-emphasis utility action.
- The future admin system owns recurring statistics, team, organizations, events, gallery items, recruitment cycles, form submissions, links, and site settings.
- Only published content appears publicly. Draft and archived records stay hidden.
- Event states include public, member-only, application-required, full, and canceled.
- Authentication, persistent form storage, dashboard structure, admin interface, and deployment infrastructure remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments

- The public name is “Traders at Carolina” everywhere.
- “CIG” and “Carolina Investment Group” are legacy names and must not appear in public copy, captions, or assets.
- The club is described as a quantitative finance club at UNC-Chapel Hill.

## Evidence on Hand

No verified statistics, executive board profiles, events, member photography, placements, sponsors, collaborators, testimonials, application URL, social URLs, or contact addresses were supplied. The product must not fabricate them. Missing material should appear as a deliberate unpublished or empty state until approved content is provided.

## Product Principles

- Explain the club before asking for commitment.
- Prove activity with verified programming and outcomes, never invented claims.
- Keep joining and partnership paths obvious from every public page.
- Let recurring content be replaceable by a future admin-backed source without rewriting page structure.
- Treat accessibility and responsive behavior as baseline product requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable navigation and carousels, visible focus states, accessible form feedback, descriptive alternative text, reduced-motion support, and sufficient contrast.
